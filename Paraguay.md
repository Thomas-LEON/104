Overview
Paraguay’s Central Bank (Banco Central del Paraguay - BCP) has officially intervened and taken control of Ueno Bank, the country's primary digital lender. The seizure followed a critical breach of solvency thresholds, freezing accounts for millions of users and unleashing acute financial and political fallout.
What We Know
 * Regulatory Seizure: The BCP board voted unanimously to place Ueno Bank into resolution after its solvency ratio (regulatory capital over risk-weighted assets) plunged below half the legal minimum. Digital services and app access were immediately suspended.
 * Scale of Disruption:
   * ~3 million depositors have been locked out of their accounts.
   * ~$380 million in public money from Paraguay's state social security and health institute (IPS) is trapped in the bank.
   * Around 32,000 retirees who relied on Ueno Bank accounts for monthly pension disbursements must be urgently rerouted to other institutions.
 * Deposit Insurance: Authorities state that roughly 97% of individual retail depositors fall within the statutory threshold covered by Paraguay’s deposit guarantee fund (Fondo de Garantía de Depósitos).
 * Root Causes: Prior to the collapse, regulators had warned Ueno Bank against excessive related-party lending—allegedly channeling customer deposits into companies linked to the bank's owners.
 * Political Fallout:
   * President Santiago Peña previously held shares in the business group controlling Ueno Bank.
   * Central Bank President Carlos Carvallo, who previously chaired Ueno Bank, resigned at Peña’s request.
   * Opposition lawmakers filed a motion for impeachment against President Peña, alleging conflict of interest and supervisory failure.
   * Vice President Pedro Alliana publicly put his 2028 presidential campaign plans on hold due to the unfolding crisis.
What Remains Uncertain / Unknown
 * Recovery of Public Assets: Whether the state pension and health fund (IPS) will be able to recover its ~$380 million investment through liquidation or legal action.
 * Payout Timeline: The operational schedule and exact process for when ordinary retail customers will actually regain access to their insured deposits.
 * Broader Contagion Risk: Whether widespread public protests and anxiety outside physical branches will trigger panic withdrawals or liquidity strains across other private banks.
 * Viability of Impeachment: Whether the opposition's impeachment push can gain enough traction, given that the ruling Colorado Party currently holds legislative majorities in both chambers of Congress.
